# Construction Subcontractor Backcharge Recovery

Document and recover costs caused by subcontractor scope failures, rework, cleanup, and schedule impacts.

**Primary buyer:** General contractors and construction managers. **Evidence:** subcontracts, scopes, daily logs, notices, labor, equipment, materials, rework, cleanup, schedule impacts, invoices, and deductions.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Subcontract scope library
- Daily log ingestion
- Issue notice tracking
- Responsible party analysis
- Labor cost reconstruction
- Equipment cost reconstruction
- Material cost reconstruction
- Rework quantity validation
- Cleanup allocation
- Schedule impact calculation
- Contract notice compliance
- Backcharge package generation
- Subcontractor response workflow
- Deduction reconciliation
- Project trade analytics
